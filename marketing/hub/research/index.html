STP Compliance Path Technical
🏛️ ATO Digital Service Provider Registration

To submit payroll data to the ATO, Tayla Payroll must register as a Digital Service Provider (DSP). The product must be STP Phase 2 compliant and pass the DSP Operational Security Framework assessment.

Shortcut: Use a Sending Service Provider (SSP) like Beam or SuperStream to handle ATO data transmission on your behalf. This dramatically reduces the compliance burden — you build the payroll UX, they handle the ATO connection.

STP Phase 2 requires: Disaggregated reporting of ordinary earnings, overtime, bonuses, allowances, leave, employment type, tax treatment codes, and termination details — not just gross figures. Budget 3–6 months for compliance work before public launch.
