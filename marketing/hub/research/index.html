STP Compliance Path Technical
🏛️ ATO Digital Service Provider Registration

To submit payroll data to the ATO, Tayla Payroll must register as a Digital Service Provider (DSP). The product must be STP Phase 2 compliant and pass the DSP Operational Security Framework assessment.

Shortcut: Use a Sending Service Provider (SSP) like Beam or SuperStream to handle ATO data transmission on your behalf. This dramatically reduces the compliance burden — you build the payroll UX, they handle the ATO connection.

STP Phase 2 requires: Disaggregated reporting of ordinary earnings, overtime, bonuses, allowances, leave, employment type, tax treatment codes, and termination details — not just gross figures. Budget 3–6 months for compliance work before public launch.

🍺 Hospitality-Specific Features

Hospitality has unique stocktake requirements — recipe-based usage tracking (a cocktail uses 45ml of gin, so 100 cocktails sold = 4.5 litres consumed), perishables management with daily counts for fresh produce, liquor licence compliance tracking, and over-pouring detection via variance reports. These are features no generic inventory app handles well. Tayla Stocktake can own this niche completely.

🛍️ Retail-Specific Features

Retail requires supplier order management based on usage trends, shrinkage alerts for high-value items, category-level reporting for buying decisions, and EOFY stock valuation for ATO compliance. 43% of Australian small businesses don't track inventory at all — Tayla Stocktake is the affordable entry point that replaces spreadsheets and manual counts.

Up Bank is a great personal finance neobank — but it's still just a bank with a nice app on top. Licensing Pathways APRA
🏛️
Banking-as-a-Service (Recommended)
Partner with an existing ADI that provides the banking infrastructure — you build the product layer. Providers like Monoova and similar BaaS platforms operate in Australia. Get accounts, cards, and payments without an ADI licence. Realistic timeline: 2–3 years. Capital required: significantly less than ADI.
📋
Full ADI Licence (Long Term)
Apply directly to APRA for a full ADI licence. New 2025 APRA framework proposes a 12-month assessment window with a 3-month decision target. Minimum $50M Tier 1 capital required. Realistic timeline: 5–8 years. Requires significant institutional backing.
⚠️
Restricted ADI (Monitor)
The RADI pathway that Up Bank and others used is currently under APRA review as of July 2025 — it may be discontinued. Only 5 of all RADI holders have successfully transitioned to full ADI. Monitor APRA's final decision before planning around this pathway.

📋 Important: APRA Naming Restrictions

APRA restricts the use of words like "bank", "banker", "banking", and even "neobank" to licensed ADIs only. Tayla cannot use "Tayla Bank" as a product name until ADI licensing is obtained or APRA consent is granted. During the BaaS phase, naming would be something like "Tayla Accounts" or "Tayla Money". Plan for this in brand and legal strategy well ahead of launch.

🚀 Recommended Path: Banking-as-a-Service First

Partner with an existing ADI via a BaaS provider. Launch as "Tayla Accounts" (APRA restricts the word "bank" to licensed ADIs — see below). Build transaction volume, user trust, and product-market fit. Accumulate capital and regulatory relationship for a full ADI application. Timeline: 2–3 years to BaaS. 5–8 years to full ADI.

⚠️ APRA Naming Restriction — Critical

APRA prohibits "bank", "banker", "banking", and "neobank" without ADI licensing. Applies to product names, marketing, and app store listings. Must be "Tayla Accounts" or "Tayla Money" during BaaS phase. Violating this is an offence under the Banking Act 1959. Plan this into brand strategy well before launch.

📋 Full ADI Requirements

Minimum $50M Tier 1 capital. APRA's 2025 framework: 12-month assessment + 3-month decision window. The Restricted ADI (RADI) pathway is currently under APRA review and may be discontinued — do not plan around it. Requires majority-independent board, full risk management framework, APRA-compliant IT systems, and external audit from day one.
