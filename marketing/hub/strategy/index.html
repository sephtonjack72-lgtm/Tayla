```
Key Technical Requirement ATO DSP Registration
🏛️ STP Phase 2 Compliance Path

Must register as an ATO Digital Service Provider (DSP). Use a Sending Service Provider (SSP) like Beam to handle ATO data transmission — dramatically reduces technical burden. STP Phase 2 requires disaggregated reporting: ordinary earnings, overtime, bonuses, allowances, employment type, leave, termination details reported separately. Budget 3–6 months for compliance work before public launch.
```

Phase 3 · 12–18 Months
Tayla Stocktake
Phone-based inventory management for retail and hospitality. Connects directly to Tayla Business P&L.
43% of Australian small businesses don't track inventory at all. For hospitality venues with no stock control, shrinkage runs at 10–20% — potentially tens of thousands of dollars lost annually. Tayla Stocktake enters a fragmented market dominated by either expensive outsourced services or tools bolted awkwardly onto POS systems like Square and Lightspeed. The differentiator is the native Tayla Business connection — stock value flows directly into P&L and BAS preparation. No manual export, no CSV upload, no reconciliation headache.
```
📱 Phone Camera Scanning
No dedicated hardware required. Use the phone camera to scan barcodes. Works offline and syncs on reconnection. Lowers the barrier to entry significantly.
📊 Variance Reporting
Expected vs actual comparison after every count. Identifies shrinkage, theft, over-pouring in hospitality. Venues with tight systems run under 2% shrinkage.
🍺 Hospitality Recipe Tracking
Recipe-based usage calculation — 100 cocktails = 4.5L gin consumed. Perishables management for daily fresh produce counts. Liquor licence compliance tracking.
🔗 Tayla Business Native Link
Stock value auto-populates cost of goods in Tayla Business P&L. BAS preparation includes inventory valuations automatically. No other affordable tool does this.
📋 ATO Legal Requirement

Australian businesses are legally required to produce evidence of at least one stocktake as close to EOFY as possible for tax purposes. This creates a guaranteed annual demand signal — every retail and hospitality business in Australia needs to do this. Tayla Stocktake makes it a 30-minute phone-based job instead of a day-long manual process.
```

```
Licensing Strategy Critical
🚀 Recommended Path: Banking-as-a-Service First

Partner with an existing ADI via a BaaS provider for the initial launch. Offer "Tayla Accounts" (not "Tayla Bank" — APRA restricts the word "bank" to licensed ADIs). Build transaction volume, user trust, and product-market fit. Use this phase to accumulate the capital and regulatory relationship needed for a full ADI application. Timeline: 2–3 years to BaaS launch. 5–8 years to full ADI.

⚠️ APRA Naming Restriction

APRA prohibits using "bank", "banker", "banking", and even "neobank" without ADI licensing. This applies to product names, marketing, and app store listings. During the BaaS phase, the product must be called something like "Tayla Accounts" or "Tayla Money" — plan this into brand strategy well before launch. Violating this is an offence under the Banking Act 1959.

📋 Full ADI Requirements

Minimum $50M Tier 1 capital for full ADI licence. APRA's new 2025 framework proposes a 12-month assessment + 3-month decision window. The Restricted ADI (RADI) pathway used by Up Bank and others is currently under APRA review and may be discontinued — do not plan around it. The direct pathway requires a majority-independent board, full risk management framework, IT systems compliant with APRA prudential standards, and external audit arrangements from day one.
```
