How It Works

From rostering to reconciliation,
in four steps.

Tayla is the only suite where a payroll run automatically onboards your staff to personal finance — zero extra effort from you.

1
Set up your business
Create your venue, add employees, and configure award rates and pay schedules in minutes.
2
Roster & track time
Build rosters, track availability and approve timesheets. Workforce calculates pay automatically.
3
Run payroll
One click runs payroll, files STP2 with the ATO, queues super, generates ABA files, and pushes payslips to every employee's Tayla app.
4
Reconcile & report
Sales sync to Tayla Business automatically. Your P&L updates in real time — no manual journal entries.
