Frequently Asked Questions
Common questions about budgeting in Australia
How much should I spend on rent in Australia?
The general rule is no more than 30% of your gross income on housing. In Sydney and Melbourne many people spend 35–40% due to high rents — if you're in this range, focus on reducing discretionary spending to compensate.
What is the 50/30/20 budget rule?
The 50/30/20 rule suggests spending 50% of after-tax income on needs (rent, food, utilities), 30% on wants (dining, entertainment, subscriptions), and saving 20%. It's a useful starting point but may need adjusting for Australian living costs.
How much should I save each month in Australia?
Financial advisors generally recommend saving at least 10–20% of your take-home pay. Even starting with $50–$100 per week builds meaningful savings. Consistency matters more than the amount.
What are average monthly expenses in Australia?
For a single person: rent $1,500–$2,500, groceries $400–$600, transport $150–$300, utilities $100–$200, dining/entertainment $300–$500. Total typically $2,500–$4,000+ depending on lifestyle and city.
