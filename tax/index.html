Frequently Asked Questions
Common questions about Australian income tax
How much tax do I pay on $80,000 in Australia?
On an $80,000 salary (2025–26) you pay approximately $16,288 in income tax plus $1,600 Medicare levy — total $17,888. Your take-home is around $62,112/year or $5,176/month. Use the calculator above for your exact figure.
What is the tax-free threshold in Australia?
The tax-free threshold is $18,200. If you earn $18,200 or less per year you pay no income tax. Above that, you're only taxed on the amount over $18,200 — at a 16% marginal rate up to $45,000.
What are the 2025–26 Australian tax brackets?
The 2025–26 marginal tax rates are: $0–$18,200 (0%), $18,201–$45,000 (16%), $45,001–$135,000 (30%), $135,001–$190,000 (37%), and $190,001+ (45%). These are marginal rates — you only pay each rate on income within that bracket, not your whole salary.
How is the Medicare levy calculated?
The Medicare levy is 2% of your taxable income. It phases in for lower incomes — no levy under $26,000, gradually increasing to the full 2% at $32,500. Toggle it on or off in the calculator above.
Does this calculator include HECS/HELP repayments?
No — this calculator covers income tax and Medicare levy only. HECS/HELP repayments depend on your individual debt balance and repayment threshold, so they're not included. Your actual take-home may be lower if you have a HECS/HELP debt.
